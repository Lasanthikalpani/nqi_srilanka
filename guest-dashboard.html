What is Quality Infrastructure?

The National Quality Infrastructure (NQI) is the framework of institutions (public and private) required to establish and implement standardization, metrology, accreditation, and conformity assessment services — testing, inspection, and certification. It ensures products and services meet defined requirements, whether imposed by regulators (technical regulations, SPS measures) or by the market.

Standardization

SLSI formulates national standards — 2,600+ standards, of which ~1,350 are ISO/IEC/ASTM/EN adoptions.

Metrology

MUSSd maintains national measurement standards traceable to SI units across 9 metrological fields.

Accreditation

SLAB accredits labs, certification bodies, and inspection bodies under ISO/IEC 17025, 17020, 17021, 17065.

Conformity Assessment

Testing, inspection, and certification services provided by accredited conformity assessment bodies.
